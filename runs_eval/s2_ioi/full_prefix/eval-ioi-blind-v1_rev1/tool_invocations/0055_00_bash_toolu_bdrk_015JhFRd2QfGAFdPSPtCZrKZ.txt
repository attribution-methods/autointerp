# Stage 1: Localization

## Objective
Identify which attention heads causally implement the IOI circuit through:
1. Logit lens to see where IO information emerges
2. Head-level activation patching to measure causal contribution

## Methods  
- Generated ABC corrupted samples (replace subject name with novel name C)
- Cached head-z activations for clean and corrupt runs (50 samples)
- Swept all 144 heads (12 layers × 12 heads) with patches at END position
- Metric: recovery = (patch_logit_diff - corrupt_logit_diff) / (clean_logit_diff - corrupt_logit_diff)
